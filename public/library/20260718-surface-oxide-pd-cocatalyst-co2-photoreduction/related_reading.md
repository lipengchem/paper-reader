# Related Reading / 相关必读

## 1. Hydroxyl-Bonded Ru on Metallic TiN Surface Catalyzing CO2 Reduction With H2O by Infrared Light

**Authors / 作者:** B. Su, Y. Kong, S. Wang, et al.  
**Journal / 期刊:** Journal of the American Chemical Society, 2023  
**DOI:** 10.1021/jacs.3c08311  

**Why this one:** This direct TiN-support predecessor is cited by the present paper. It establishes why metallic TiN can participate in H2O-coupled CO2 reduction and therefore gives the support-side baseline before adding the Pd/PdOx cocatalyst interface.

**为什么推荐：** 这篇文献是本文直接引用的 TiN 支撑体前置工作。它建立了金属 TiN 参与 H2O 耦合 CO2 还原的基础逻辑，因此可作为理解“在 TiN 上再引入 Pd/PdOx 界面”之前的支撑体基线。

**Suggested order / 建议阅读顺序:** Read this paper first for the TiN platform, then return to the present reader to focus on how Pd/PdOx spatially separates proton collection from CO2 activation.
