# Related Reading / 相关阅读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Tuckerman, M. E., Marx, D. & Parrinello, M. The nature and transport mechanism of hydrated hydroxide ions in aqueous solution. Nature 417, 925-929 (2002).

**Why this one:** This is the direct historical mechanism paper that the 2018 Nature Chemistry work revisits with improved electronic-structure treatment.

**为什么推荐：** 这是 2018 年 Nature Chemistry 工作重新审视的直接历史机制论文，改进了电子结构处理。

2. Agmon, N. et al. Protons and Hydroxide Ions in Aqueous Systems. Chemical Reviews 116, 7642-7672 (2016).

**Why this one:** The review gives the broader experimental and theoretical background needed to see why the hydronium/hydroxide asymmetry matters.

**为什么推荐：** 该综述提供了了解水合氢离子/氢氧化物不对称性为何重要所需的更广泛的实验和理论背景。
