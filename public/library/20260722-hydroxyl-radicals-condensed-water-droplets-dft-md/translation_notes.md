## Translation conventions / 术语约定

- OH* denotes the hydroxyl radical and is not silently changed to OH− or ·OH.
- `accommodation` is translated as “容纳/稳定停留”; `hemibond` as “半键”; and `condensed water droplet` as “凝并水滴”.
- References are retained as original bibliographic metadata. Their English titles are not artificially paraphrased, while their relevance is explained in the Chinese source-map note.
