# Related Reading / 相关必读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Li, Y.-F., Liu, Z.-P., Liu, L. & Gao, W. **Mechanism and Activity of Photocatalytic Oxygen Evolution on Titania Anatase in Aqueous Surroundings.** *Journal of the American Chemical Society* **132**, 13008-13015 (2010).

**中文题名 / 中文解释:** 水环境中锐钛矿二氧化钛光催化析氧的机理与活性。

**Why this one:** This is the direct theoretical predecessor cited by Chen et al. for the OER energetics and the identification of the first PCET as the rate-determining step on anatase TiO2. Reading it first or next makes clear what the 2013 paper adds: explicit chemical dynamics, solvent-configuration sampling, the ordering of PT and ET, and the shared-hole pathway.

**为什么推荐:** 这是 Chen 等人直接依赖的理论前作：它给出了锐钛矿 TiO2 上 OER 的能量学，并把第一个 PCET 识别为速率决定步骤。先读或接着读它，可以清楚看出 2013 年本文新增了什么：显式化学动力学、溶剂构型采样、PT 与 ET 的先后顺序，以及共享空穴路径。

**Decision / 推荐决策:** No additional paper is force-listed. Other cited experiments and reviews are useful context, but they are not as directly prerequisite to the central mechanistic advance.

**中文说明:** 今天不再强行增加其他推荐。其余实验和综述可作为背景，但对理解本文核心机理推进并没有这篇前作那么不可替代。
