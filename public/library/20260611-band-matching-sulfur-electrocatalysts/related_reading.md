# Related Reading

## Decision / 推荐决策

**English:** Two strongly recommended background papers today.

**中文说明:** 今天保留两篇强相关必读文献，因为它们分别补齐“反应网络图谱”和“破除标度关系的通用背景”，不是为了凑数。

## 1. Establishing Reaction Networks in the 16-Electron Sulfur Reduction Reaction

**中文题名 / 中文解释:** 建立 16 电子硫还原反应的完整反应网络

**Authors / Venue:** Liu et al.; Nature (2024)

**Why this one:** This is a must-read precursor if you want to understand why pathway-level descriptors matter in Li-S chemistry. It maps the sulfur-reduction network itself, so today's band-matching paper can be read as a descriptor-level answer built on that mechanistic landscape.

**为什么推荐:** 如果想真正理解为什么 Li-S 体系需要“路径级描述符”，这篇 2024 年 Nature 几乎绕不开。它把 16 电子硫还原网络本身梳理清楚，而今天这篇 JACS 可以看成是在那个机理地图之上再往前走一步，给出一个可预测的电子结构设计规则。

**Suggested order:** Read this first for the mechanistic map, then come back to today's descriptor paper.

**建议阅读顺序:** 建议先读它来建立机理地图，再回来看今天这篇描述符论文。

## 2. Breaking the Linear Scaling Limit in Multi-Electron-Transfer Electrocatalysis through Intermediate Spillover

**中文题名 / 中文解释:** 通过中间体溢流打破多电子转移电催化中的线性标度极限

**Authors / Venue:** Wang et al.; Nature Catalysis (2025)

**Why this one:** This is the most directly relevant general background on how multielectron electrocatalysis can escape scaling limits. Today's paper cites it as part of the broader motivation, but then translates that idea into the specific sulfur-redox context and into a band-structure descriptor.

**为什么推荐:** 这篇文献是理解“多电子电催化如何跳出标度极限”最直接的通用背景之一。今天这篇 JACS 把类似思想转译到了硫氧化还原场景，并把它落到了能带结构描述符上。

**Suggested order:** Read this after the 2024 Nature paper to compare a general scaling-break strategy with today's sulfur-specific one.

**建议阅读顺序:** 建议放在 2024 年 Nature 之后阅读，用来比较“通用破标度策略”和今天的“硫体系专属规则”。
