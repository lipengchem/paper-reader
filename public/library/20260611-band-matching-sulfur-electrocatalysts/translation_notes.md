# Translation Notes

## Extraction Status / 抽取状态
- Source type: selectable-text publisher PDF.
- OCR was not required.
- Six embedded main-figure images were extracted into `assets/` and placed near the first substantive discussion blocks.

## Scope Decisions / 范围说明
- Main text, abstract, conclusion, associated-content note, author information, notes, and acknowledgements were retained in bilingual form.
- The reference list was not translated entry by entry. It is treated as bibliography metadata only, consistent with the task rule.
- Supporting Information details were summarized from the article's own associated-content section rather than reconstructed from the SI PDF.

## Layout Notes / 版式说明
- This reader follows the semantic logic of the paper rather than the exact two-column JACS layout.
- Several continuous ideas split across page breaks in the publisher PDF were merged into stable reader blocks to keep the bilingual flow readable.
- Figure assets are extracted embedded article figures, not screenshot crops of the entire page.

## Uncertainty / 不确定性
- The exact closed-form formula for the band-matching ratio is delegated to Supplementary Note 5 in the paper; this reader explains its ingredients and interpretation but does not recreate every algebraic detail.
- Numerical values reported here were kept from the article text where available. Minor graphical values visible only in supplementary panels were not independently re-measured.
- Related reading items were chosen from the paper's own conceptual backbone and title-level citation context; DOI and local Zotero presence were intentionally not fabricated.

## Skipped Or Compressed Content / 省略或压缩内容
- The full reference pages (pp.11-13) were not expanded into bilingual paragraph pairs.
- Supporting figures and tables were not individually reproduced.
