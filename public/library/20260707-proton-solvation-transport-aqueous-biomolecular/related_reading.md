# Related Reading / 相关阅读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Marx, D., Tuckerman, M. E., Hutter, J. & Parrinello, M. The nature of the hydrated excess proton in water. Nature 397, 601-604 (1999).

**Why this one:** This is the classic ab initio molecular dynamics paper behind modern microscopic thinking about hydrated excess-proton structure.

**为什么推荐：** 这是关于水合过量质子结构的现代微观思维背后的经典从头算分子动力学论文。

2. Tuckerman, M. E., Marx, D. & Parrinello, M. The nature and transport mechanism of hydrated hydroxide ions in aqueous solution. Nature 417, 925-929 (2002).

**Why this one:** Reading it alongside Swanson et al. makes the proton/hydroxide asymmetry and the MS-EVB/ab initio contrast much clearer.

**为什么推荐：** 与斯旺森等人一起阅读。使质子/氢氧化物不对称性和 MS-EVB/ab 从头对比更加清晰。
