# Related Reading / 相关阅读

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Marx, D., Tuckerman, M. E., Hutter, J. & Parrinello, M. **The nature of the hydrated excess proton in water.** Nature 397, 601-604 (1999).

**Why this one:** This is the direct hydronium-side counterpart to today's hydroxide paper. It establishes the proton-transfer language and quantum simulation background that the 2002 paper explicitly contrasts against.

**为什么推荐:** 这是今天这篇 OH- 论文的 H3O+ 对照篇。2002 年这篇文章很多地方都是在反驳“OH- 只是 H3O+ 反向过程”的直觉，所以先读或补读这篇能把对照关系看清楚。

2. Agmon, N. **Mechanism of hydroxide mobility.** Chemical Physics Letters 319, 247-252 (2000).

**Why this one:** This is the immediate mechanistic context cited by the paper for hydroxide mobility and activation energetics, and it helps explain what the authors are revising.

**为什么推荐:** 这是本文讨论 OH- 迁移和活化能时直接面对的机制背景。读它可以看出 Tuckerman 等人在修正哪一种已有图像。
