# Related Reading / 相关必读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Xie, T., Fu, X., Ganea, O.-E., Barzilay, R. & Jaakkola, T. S. **Crystal diffusion variational autoencoder for periodic material generation.** Proc. International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR, 2022).

**中文题名:** 用于周期性材料生成的晶体扩散变分自编码器。

**Why this one:** MatterGen repeatedly uses CDVAE as a direct prior state-of-the-art/baseline for periodic material generation. Reading it first or next helps clarify what MatterGen changes: from VAE-style periodic crystal generation toward a joint diffusion process over atom types, coordinates and lattice with broader conditional control.

**为什么推荐这一篇:** MatterGen 在文中反复把 CDVAE 作为周期性材料生成领域的直接前作和先进基线。先读或接着读这篇，可以帮助你看清 MatterGen 到底改进了什么：它从 VAE 风格的周期晶体生成，推进到对原子类型、坐标和晶格进行联合扩散建模，并且具备更宽泛的条件控制能力。

No other related papers are force-listed today; the rest are useful context but not unavoidable for understanding this paper's central methodological advance.

**今日不强行推荐其他相关必读文献:** 其余文献可以作为背景材料，但对于理解这篇论文的核心方法进展，并不是绕不开的前置论文或关键基线。
