# Translation and extraction notes / 翻译与抽取说明

- **Source:** selectable-text publisher PDF, 12 pages; the copied `paper.pdf` is byte-identical to the Zotero attachment (SHA-256 `e701bb1b5b6b27aed6eb99d28756b85b713d436b07373d382d3345486c10e37e`).
- **Layout:** ACS two-column pages were reconstructed by column before translation. Page headers, download/sharing boilerplate, author-address blocks, and the bibliography were excluded; experimental methods, captions, conclusions, acknowledgments, and the acknowledgment continuation immediately before the references were retained.
- **Formulas and symbols:** PDF text extraction flattens some equation layout, superscripts/subscripts, isotope labels, and defect notation. These are preserved as extractable text where possible; consult the original PDF for typographic equation layout.
- **Figures:** six tightly cropped visual cards (Figs. 1-3 and Schemes 1-3) were rendered from their source pages. Captions remain as source-map blocks and are shown bilingually with each card.
- **OCR:** none used; all text was extracted from the embedded text layer.
- **Uncertainty:** XAFS temperature inference and the distinction between photothermal and charge-separation effects are the authors' evidence chain, not a universal equivalence between local heating and a mechanism.
