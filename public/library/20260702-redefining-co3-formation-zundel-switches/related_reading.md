# Related Reading / 相关阅读

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Buxton, G. V.; Greenstock, C. L.; Helman, W. P.; Ross, A. B. **Critical review of rate constants for reactions of hydrated electrons, hydrogen atoms and hydroxyl radicals (·OH/·O−) in aqueous solution.** *J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data* 1988, 17, 513-886.

**Why this one:** This is the foundational kinetic reference behind many bulk-solution ·OH scavenging and carbonate-radical arguments. Reading it helps you see exactly what the new JACS paper is pushing against: not that the old rate constants are useless, but that bulk-rate logic is incomplete at interfaces.

**为什么推荐这一篇：** 这是水相 ·OH、氢原子和水合电子反应速率常数的基础参考，许多“碳酸盐猝灭 ·OH / 生成 ·CO3−”的均相讨论都建立在这一类数据上。读它能帮你分清这篇 JACS 的创新点：不是否定旧速率常数，而是指出均相体相逻辑在界面条件下不完整。
