# Related Reading / 相关阅读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Tuckerman, M. E., Marx, D. & Parrinello, M. The nature and transport mechanism of hydrated hydroxide ions in aqueous solution. Nature 417, 925-929 (2002).

**Why this one:** This paper sets the structural-diffusion problem that the reorientation mechanism paper tries to explain in classical-MD language.

**为什么推荐：** 本文提出了重定向机制论文试图用经典MD语言解释的结构扩散问题。

2. Chen, M. et al. Hydroxide diffuses slower than hydronium in water because its solvated structure inhibits correlated proton transfer. Nature Chemistry 10, 413-419 (2018).

**Why this one:** It is the later electronic-structure benchmark for judging which hydroxide reorientation pictures remain physically plausible.

**为什么推荐：** 它是后来的电子结构基准，用于判断哪些氢氧化物重新取向图在物理上仍然合理。
