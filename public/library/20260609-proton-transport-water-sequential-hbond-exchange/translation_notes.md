# Translation Notes

## Extraction Status / 抽取状态

- Source type: selectable-text publisher PDF.
- OCR was not required.
- Four main-figure page-region crops were generated into `assets/` and placed near the first substantive discussion points.

## Scope Decisions / 范围说明

- Main text, figure captions, Methods, acknowledgements, author contributions, competing interests, data availability and extended-data summaries were retained in bilingual form.
- The full reference list was not translated entry by entry. It remains in the source PDF and is recorded here as intentionally not expanded paragraph-by-paragraph.
- Extended Data figures were summarized in prose rather than inserted one by one as separate image blocks, to keep the reader focused and readable.

## Layout Notes / 版式说明

- This is a full bilingual reading artifact, not a pixel-faithful reconstruction of the Nature Chemistry PDF layout.
- Figure crops are semantic page-region screenshots. They are tight enough for reading, but still approximate compared with manual vector-level extraction.

## Uncertainty / 不确定性

- Some symbols extracted from the PDF use normalized plain-text forms, especially `ΔE`, `ω01`, `ω12`, superscripts, subscripts and minus signs.
- Page text extraction contains occasional publisher-glyph artifacts in the raw PDF text. These were normalized in the bilingual prose for readability.
- Abstracts stored in Zotero metadata were incomplete for several candidate papers, so selection leaned on title, journal, collection context and direct PDF content instead of Zotero abstract fields alone.

## Recommendation Notes / 推荐说明

- The two related-reading recommendations were chosen because they serve as the clearest "before" and "experimental challenge" context for today's mechanism paper.
- Their Zotero presence was not confirmed during this run, so they are marked `未确认` instead of being claimed as already present in the library.
