# Related Reading

## Decision / 推荐决策

**English:** Two strongly recommended background papers today.

**中文:** 今天有两篇值得明确列为“强推荐背景必读”的文献。

## 1. The nature of the hydrated excess proton in water

**中文题名/说明:** 水中水合过量质子的本性

**Authors / Venue:** Marx, Tuckerman, Hutter and Parrinello; Nature (1999)

**Zotero status / Zotero 状态:** 未确认

**Why this one:** This is the classic ab initio simulation paper that established the dominant Eigen-basin picture with Zundel-like transfer events. Today's paper is best read as a careful revision of that canonical framework rather than an unrelated replacement.

**为什么推荐:** 这是建立“稳定 Eigen 盆地 + Zundel 式转移事件”经典图景的代表性从头算分子动力学论文。今天这篇 Nature Chemistry 最适合被理解为对那套标准图景的精细修订，而不是另起炉灶。

**Suggested order:** Read this first if you want the historical baseline.

**建议阅读顺序:** 如果你想先建立历史基线，建议最先读它。

## 2. Ultrafast 2D IR spectroscopy of the excess proton in liquid water

**中文题名/说明:** 液态水中过量质子的超快二维红外光谱

**Authors / Venue:** Thämer, De Marco, Ramasesha, Mandal and Tokmakoff; Science (2015)

**Zotero status / Zotero 状态:** 未确认

**Why this one:** This is a key experimental trigger for the present work. It is one of the studies that argued for long-lived Zundel-like signatures and therefore forced theorists to revisit the one-stable-state picture.

**为什么推荐:** 这是直接推动本文出现的重要实验触发点之一。它给出了长寿命类 Zundel 信号的证据，迫使理论模型重新审视“只有一个稳定态”的旧图景。

**Suggested order:** Read this after the 1999 Nature paper to see why the older mechanism became insufficient.

**建议阅读顺序:** 建议放在 1999 年 Nature 之后读，这样能更直观看到旧机制为什么开始不够用了。
