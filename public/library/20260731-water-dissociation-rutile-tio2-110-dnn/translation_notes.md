# Translation and extraction notes / 翻译与抽取说明

- Source format: seven-page selectable-text PDF; no OCR was applied.
- Each source-map block preserves one selectable-text block. Two-column blocks were ordered by column but were never automatically merged.
- Four embedded figure regions were rendered at 2x. Their crop boxes are intentionally tight around the visual content; captions are separately source-anchored and bilingual.
- Mathematical typography, superscripts/subscripts, hyphenation, and the PDF's ligature/spacing layer can differ from the visual original. Check `paper.pdf` before reusing exact equations, units, or symbols.
- Methods, data/software availability, and acknowledgments before the bibliographic list were retained. References were kept as bibliographic information only and not translated entry by entry.
- Translation is source-anchored automatic first-pass Chinese. Technical terms and named methods were preserved; dense method prose should be manually checked against the original for quotation-quality reuse.
