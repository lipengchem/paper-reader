# Related Reading / 延伸阅读

## Strong recommendation / 强推荐

**Free energy of proton transfer at the water-TiO2 interface from ab initio deep potential molecular dynamics** — Calegari Andrade et al., *Chemical Science* (2020), DOI: 10.1039/c9sc05116c.

**Why this one:** This is the explicitly cited aqueous-anatase TiO2(101) counterpart and uses the same broad DPMD logic, making it the essential comparison for the rutile result.

**为什么推荐：** 这是本文明确引用的水相锐钛矿 TiO2(101) 对照工作，且采用同一类 DPMD 逻辑；它是理解本文金红石结果的必要比较基准。
