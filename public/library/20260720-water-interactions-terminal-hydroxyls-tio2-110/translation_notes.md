# Translation Notes

## Extraction Status / 抽取状态
- Source type: selectable-text publisher PDF; no OCR was used.
- The full five-page article was processed. The numbered bibliography was retained as bibliographic metadata rather than duplicated as bilingual prose.
- Six main figures were tightly cropped from their own visual regions; captions remain separate and bilingual in `paper.md`.

## Terminology Decisions / 术语统一
- OHt = terminal hydroxyl = 端位羟基; OHb = bridging hydroxyl = 桥位羟基; Oa = oxygen adatom = 氧吸附原子; VO = oxygen vacancy = 氧空位.
- "Apparent motion" is translated as "表观移动" to distinguish a measured STM displacement from literal diffusion of the same oxygen atom.

## Layout Notes / 版式说明
- The source uses two columns; paragraphs that cross a column or page boundary were rejoined before translation.
- Figure cards are inserted after the first substantive paragraph that interprets each figure, not collected at the front or end.

## Uncertainty / 不确定性
- The paper reports a flat transition-state region for some water-hopping steps; the translation preserves this limitation and does not claim a uniquely resolved transition-state geometry.
- References are not translated line by line because they are citation metadata, not extractable body prose.
