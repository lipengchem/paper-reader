# Related Reading

## Why These Papers

Only two papers are recommended today because both are genuinely foundational for following up this ACS Catalysis perspective. No extra similar-title list is added.

今日只推荐两篇，因为它们都属于读完这篇 ACS Catalysis Perspective 之后真正值得继续追的基础文献；不额外堆相似标题。

## 1. Machine Learning Force Fields

**中文题名 / Chinese title:** 《机器学习力场》

**Authors / Venue:** Unke et al., Chemical Reviews, 2021

**Zotero status:** 未确认

**Why this one:** This is the broadest authoritative review cited by the Perspective. It is the best follow-up if you want a systematic map of architectures, training strategies, uncertainty, and benchmark culture beyond the catalysis examples in today's paper.

**为什么推荐：** 这是本文引用的最权威、也最全面的方法综述之一。如果你读完今天这篇想把架构类型、训练流程、不确定性估计和 benchmark 文化系统补起来，它几乎是最稳妥的下一篇。

**Suggested order:** Read this after today's Perspective if you want the full method landscape.

**建议阅读顺序：** 如果你想先补全方法全景，再往具体体系里钻，这篇建议排在今天这篇之后立刻读。

## 2. Machine learning potentials for extended systems - a perspective

**中文题名 / Chinese title:** 《扩展体系中的机器学习势：一篇 Perspective》

**Authors / Venue:** Behler and Csanyi, European Physical Journal B, 2021

**Zotero status:** 未确认

**Why this one:** Today's article is catalysis-centered. This companion perspective is especially useful for understanding how extended condensed-phase systems change the design priorities of MLPs, including locality, symmetry handling, and scaling.

**为什么推荐：** 今天这篇更偏催化应用，而这篇 companion perspective 更适合帮助你理解“扩展凝聚态体系为什么会改变 MLP 设计重点”，尤其是局域性、对称性和可扩展性这些底层问题。

**Suggested order:** Read this after the Chemical Reviews article if you want a shorter, systems-oriented consolidation.

**建议阅读顺序：** 如果你先读了上面的 Chemical Reviews，这篇适合接着读，用更聚焦扩展体系的视角做一次收束。
