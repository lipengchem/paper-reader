# Translation Notes

## Extraction Status

- Source type: selectable-text publisher PDF.
- OCR was not required.
- Two embedded article figures were extracted into `assets/` and placed near the first discussion point where they matter.

## Scope Decisions

- The full argumentative body, method overview, application survey, discussion, conclusion, author information, competing-interest statement, and acknowledgements are represented in bilingual form.
- Reference pages were not translated item by item. They remain in the source PDF for lookup, which matches the task rule that bibliography metadata need not be translated paragraph by paragraph.
- Short formula-only fragments such as the HDNNP total-energy expression were absorbed into the surrounding prose explanation instead of being preserved as standalone equation blocks.

## Layout And Fidelity

- The original article is a Perspective with dense two-column pages. For readability, continuous ideas that were split across columns or pages in the PDF were merged into stable reader blocks.
- The bilingual reader is source-grounded but not a visual facsimile of the ACS layout.
- Names of model families such as HDNNP, DeePMD, MACE, ACE, and MPNN were kept in English abbreviations to match the literature.

## Uncertainty

- Some publisher line-break artifacts such as `water-TiO2` versus `water-TiO2` and page-break continuations were normalized for readability.
- Related-reading availability in the local Zotero library was not re-verified item by item; both recommendations were made from the article's own reference backbone and may require later Zotero confirmation.
