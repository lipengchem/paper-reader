# Related Reading

## Strongly Recommended / 强烈推荐

1. Zhu, Z. et al. **Atomic-level design of dual-metal-site catalysts for oxygen reduction.**

**Why this one:** Read one representative dual-site ORR design paper if you need background for why distance, coordination, and electronic asymmetry matter in ORR.

**为什么推荐：** 如果需要补双位点 ORR 设计背景，建议读一篇代表性的双金属位点 ORR 文章，重点理解距离、配位环境和电子不对称为什么会影响中间体吸附。
