# Related Reading / 相关必读

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Wang, D.; Sheng, T.; Chen, J.; Wang, H.-F.; Hu, P. **Identifying the Key Obstacle in Photocatalytic Oxygen Evolution on Rutile TiO2.** *Nature Catalysis* **2018**, *1*, 291-299. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41929-018-0065-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41929-018-0065-z)

**Why this one / 为什么推荐：** The present paper explicitly uses this rutile-TiO2 mechanistic framework as its close precursor. Read it to establish what makes the rutile water-oxidation landscape difficult before comparing why the brookite interface redirects the OH/O balance and the O-O coupling step toward H2O2.
