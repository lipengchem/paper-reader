# Translation Notes

## Extraction Status

- Source type: selectable-text publisher PDF.
- OCR was not required.
- Three page-region crops were generated into `assets/` and placed near the first substantive discussion point.

## Scope Decisions

- The argumentative body, model construction, figure captions, encapsulation criterion, correction note, acknowledgements, data availability, and license-related text were retained in bilingual form.
- The reference list was not translated item by item. It remains in the source PDF and is recorded here as intentionally not expanded paragraph-by-paragraph.
- Two-column text and page breaks were merged into stable logical blocks so the reader follows the scientific argument rather than the publisher layout.

## Layout Notes

- This is a full bilingual reader, but not a pixel-faithful replica of the Science page layout.
- Figure crops are page-region screenshots rather than isolated vector reassembly; they are semantically placed and readable, but should be treated as approximate crops.

## Correction Note

- The article carries a correction dated 13 December 2024. The corrected values `c1 = 9.85 × 10^-2/eV` and `c2 = -3.06 × 10^-3/Å^2` were used in the reading notes.

## Uncertainty

- Some symbols in the PDF extraction, especially prime marks and superscripts in equations, are normalized in prose form for readability.
- Figure captions were condensed slightly while keeping the scientific meaning, because raw publisher extraction included broken glyphs and layout artifacts.
