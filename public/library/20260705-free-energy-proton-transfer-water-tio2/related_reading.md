# Related Reading / 相关阅读

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Zhang, L., Han, J., Wang, H., Car, R. & E, W. **Deep Potential Molecular Dynamics: A Scalable Model with the Accuracy of Quantum Mechanics.** Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 143001 (2018).

**Why this one:** This is the direct methodological foundation for the DPMD strategy used here. Reading it helps clarify why a neural-network potential can extend AIMD-quality sampling to nanosecond-scale oxide-water interface simulations.

**为什么推荐这一篇:** 这是本文 DPMD 方法的直接基础。先读或后读它，可以帮助理解为什么神经网络势函数能把 AIMD 级别精度推进到纳秒尺度的氧化物-水界面采样。
