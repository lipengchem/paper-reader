# Related Reading

## Strongly Recommended / 强烈推荐

1. Li, X. et al. **Integrated photothermal chemistry based on solar energy: photothermal catalytic water splitting and environmental catalysis.**

**Why this one:** This line of work is the experimental/photothermal background for asking why steam-water or bubble-water interfaces can alter photocatalytic water-splitting rates.

**为什么推荐：** 这类光热催化工作是理解本文问题来源的背景：为什么蒸汽-水或气泡-水界面会让水分解速率显著改变。
