# Related Reading / 相关阅读文献

## Strongly recommended / 强推荐

1. Agmon, N. The Grotthuss mechanism. Chemical Physics Letters 244, 456-462 (1995).

**Why this one:** This is a compact conceptual precursor for the proton-transfer language that the Chemical Reviews article systematizes.

**为什么推荐：** 这是化学评论文章系统化的质子转移语言的紧凑概念先驱。

2. Tuckerman, M. E., Marx, D. & Parrinello, M. The nature and transport mechanism of hydrated hydroxide ions in aqueous solution. Nature 417, 925-929 (2002).

**Why this one:** It is one of the essential microscopic hydroxide-transport reference points repeatedly contextualized by this review.

**为什么推荐：** 它是本综述反复提及的重要的微观氢氧根传输参考点之一。
